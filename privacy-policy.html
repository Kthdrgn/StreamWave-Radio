Privacy Policy — StreamWave Radio

Effective date: July 28, 2026

StreamWave Radio ("the app") is developed and published by Keith Dragon ("we," "us"). This policy explains what information is and is not collected when you use the app, and how it is handled.

The short version: StreamWave Radio has no user accounts and we do not collect, store, or sell any personal information ourselves. Your favorites, playlists, and listening history stay on your device. The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob, which collects certain data described below.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • The app does not require or offer account registration. We never ask for your name, email address, or any other personal details.
  • Your favorite stations, saved playlists, listening history, presets, and settings are stored only in a local database on your device. They are never transmitted to us.
  • Your local music library is scanned entirely on your device (see "Local Music" below). No information about your music files is ever uploaded anywhere.
  • The app contains no analytics or tracking SDKs operated by us.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the app displays advertisements served by Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service operated by Google LLC. To serve and measure ads, the AdMob SDK may collect:

  • Device identifiers, including your device's advertising ID
  • Approximate location, estimated from your IP address
  • Ad interaction data, such as which ads were shown and tapped
  • Diagnostic and performance data related to ad delivery

This data is collected by and shared with Google under Google's own policies, for the purposes of advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. We do not receive or store this data. For details, see:

  • Google's Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services

Your choices: You can reset or delete your advertising ID, and opt out of ad personalization, in your device's settings under Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Internet Radio Streams

When you play a radio station, your device connects directly to that station's streaming server, which is operated by a third-party broadcaster. Like any internet connection, the broadcaster's server can see your IP address. We do not operate these servers and are not responsible for their privacy practices.

App Content Delivery

The app's station directory, curated playlists, and related catalog content are delivered from our backend servers. When the app fetches this content, your IP address is processed transiently as a technical necessity of the connection, like any web request. We do not log, store, or link this information to you.

Local Music

If you choose to use the local music player, the app requests permission to read audio files on your device (READ_MEDIA_AUDIO). This permission is used solely to find and play your music. Scanning happens entirely on your device, and no information about your files leaves it. You can decline this permission and still use all radio features.

Backup and Restore

The app's export feature creates a backup file of your favorites, playlists, and presets. This file is saved only to the location you choose on your device. It is never sent to us or any third party.

Chromecast and Android Auto

If you cast audio to a Chromecast device or use the app through Android Auto, playback is handled by Google's frameworks on your own devices. Google's terms and privacy policy apply to those services.

Data Retention and Deletion

Because we do not collect your data, there is nothing for us to retain or delete. All app data on your device can be removed at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. Data collected by Google AdMob is subject to Google's retention policies; you can manage it through your device's ad settings and your Google account.

Children

StreamWave Radio is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

All connections between the app and its content servers, and between the app and Google's advertising services, use HTTPS encryption.

Changes to This Policy

If we change this policy — for example, if a future version of the app adds features that involve new data practices — we will update this page and revise the effective date above.
